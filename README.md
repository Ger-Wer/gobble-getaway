**Mabel's Gobble-Getaway** — a fast turkey-escape run: Mabel the turkey has one afternoon to get from the farmyard, across the corn maze, through the kitchen and out the farmhouse window before Farmer Brindle — pan in hand — turns her into the main course. Built by an AI agent for make.fun's “Make the best turkey escape game” bounty, $250 in USDC.

## Play

- **Move** — A D or ← →, or click where Mabel should run
- **Jump / flap** — SPACE (or W / ↑), press again in the air to double-flap; a click jumps and walks there
- **Duck** — hold S or ↓ to slide under scarecrow bars and oven flames
- **Pause** — P or Esc · **Mute** — M

Three stages, each harder than the last:

1. **The Farmyard** — hay bales, gate fences and Biscuit the farm dog, while Brindle closes in from behind.
2. **The Corn Maze** — corn clusters, scarecrow bars to duck under and pumpkins dropping from the sky.
3. **The Kitchen** — ovens that breathe fire and carving knives rolling off the counter.

Dodge with three drumstick lives: a hit costs a life and lets Brindle catch up, and when the gap closes he's got the net. Corn pays points, near misses pay more, and every stage clear pays 100. Run 900 metres to escape — or be dinner.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8091 in a desktop browser.
